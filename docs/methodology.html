Pipeline Design

Why Steam is the Spine

Steam is the dominant PC distribution platform for idle games and provides: structured game metadata, user-derived tags (theme, mechanic, art style), review counts and percentages, and price/business model data. SteamSpy provides modeled owner counts which, despite their uncertainty, are the only freely available proxy for reach on the platform.

Weighted Score Formula

weighted_score = review_pct_positive × log(1 + total_owners)
This balances quality (review%) with reach (owners). Biases toward popular titles — a niche game with 100% reviews will score lower than a popular game with 85%. Owner counts use SteamSpy estimates (medium confidence).

Theme / Mechanic / Art Style Classification

Tags are parsed from Steam store pages using a predefined taxonomy. A game may belong to multiple themes or mechanics. Tag presence is binary (present/absent); no weighting by tag position or steam-tag votes.

Underserved Flag (Gaps Module)

A theme is flagged underserved when: supply ≤ 25th percentile AND demand ≥ median demand. Thresholds are corpus-relative — adding more games shifts the percentile. Treat as directional signal, not a hard market gap.

Update Cadence

Active: ≤ 30 days since last detected update. Occasional: 31–120 days. Abandoned: > 120 days or no update date found. Update dates are scraped from Steam — missing dates default to "abandoned".

Exclusions

  • Mobile (iOS/Android): No free/reliable structured data source.
  • SteamDB: Excluded from this pipeline.
  • News API: Excluded from this pipeline.
  • Reddit sentiment: r/incremental_games top.json returns 403 without OAuth; not collected unless cached data is present.

Known Limitations & Caveats

  • Steam is the quantitative spine. Owner counts are SteamSpy modeled estimates — not verified sales figures. Treat as order-of-magnitude only.
  • Mobile (iOS/Android) is not covered — no free/reliable data source was available.
  • Reddit r/incremental_games top.json returns 403 without OAuth; qualitative sentiment not collected unless cached.
  • Kongregate ratings are 1–5 stars mapped to a percentage; they are NOT comparable to Steam review percentages (low confidence).
  • itch.io provides no owner/revenue data — coverage counts only.
  • Scrapers parse static HTML/JSON-LD; counts reflect one page of results, not the full catalogue.
  • SteamDB and News API were excluded from this pipeline.
  • Weighted score = review_pct_positive × log(1 + total_owners); biases toward popular games with high approval.
  • itch.io provides no owner/revenue data — coverage counts only.
  • Kongregate ratings are 1-5 stars mapped to %positive (low confidence); not comparable to Steam review %.
  • Mobile stores not covered (no free data source).
  • itch.io and Kongregate data are scraped from static HTML / JSON-LD; counts reflect one page of results, not full catalogue.
